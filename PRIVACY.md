# Chika Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

Chika is a comic reader for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac. It runs entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** Chika collects, stores, transmits, and shares **no personal data whatsoever**.

- Chika has **no network access**. The Android app declares no `INTERNET` permission, the iPhone and iPad app makes no network requests, and the Mac app's sandbox does not grant network access. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.
- Comics you import are copied into the app's private storage on your device and are readable only by Chika.
- On a Mac you can also read comics in place, or have Chika watch a folder. Chika then keeps a bookmark to the files or folder you chose so it can open them again. It only reads them, and never changes, moves or uploads them.
- Reading progress, bookmarks, panel corrections, library metadata, and settings are stored locally on your device only.
- Panel detection runs entirely on-device using a bundled machine-learning model. No page images or derived data are ever uploaded anywhere.
- Chika contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

## Mac features that use the system

- **Translation of speech balloons** reads the text with Apple's on-device text recognition and translates it with Apple's on-device Translation framework. If a language is not installed yet, macOS offers to download it from Apple; macOS handles that download, and no comic text or images are sent with it.
- **Read aloud** uses the speech voices built into macOS, on your Mac.
- **Finder thumbnails** for comic files are drawn on your Mac by Chika's Quick Look extension.
- **Shortcuts and Siri actions** (next panel, open last comic, and similar) run inside the app on your Mac.
- **Export Panel Corrections** writes the panels you corrected by hand to a folder you pick. Nothing is exported unless you choose to.

## Data sharing

We share no data with anyone, because we have none.

## Data deletion

On Android, iPhone and iPad, uninstalling the app deletes everything Chika stores: imported comic copies, covers, reading progress, and settings.

On a Mac, removing a comic from the library deletes Chika's imported copy but never a file you read in place. To delete everything Chika stores, delete the app and the folder `~/Library/Containers/com.chakra.comicreader`.

## External links

The app's menu contains a link to the project's GitHub page. Opening it hands off to your browser; the standard privacy terms of GitHub then apply.

## Children

Chika does not collect data from anyone, including children.
