What Chika does

FROM PAGETO PANEL.

One tap moves the story forward. A swipe takes you to the next page.

YOUR COMICS.YOUR PACE.

Bring your library

Open CBZ and CBR files. Chika remembers the page and panel where you stopped.

Read either way

Switch between left-to-right and right-to-left reading. Pan a panel or return to the whole page whenever you like.

Stay offline

Panel detection happens on your device. Your comics do not need a connection to be read.
